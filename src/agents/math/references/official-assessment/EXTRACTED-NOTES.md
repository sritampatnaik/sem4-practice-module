## SEAB-4052 — O-Level Mathematics syllabus (examination 2026)

Source: https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/334/fece62fa-b6d4-4daf-ab47-96c9c168b51b/4052_y26_sy.pdf

**Assessment objectives:** AO1 applying techniques 45% · AO2 solving problems 40% ·
AO3 reasoning/communication 15%.

**Scheme of assessment:** two papers, 90 marks each (50/50).
- Paper 1 — 2h15m, ~26 short-answer questions.
- Paper 2 — 2h15m, 9–10 varying-length questions, real-world-context emphasis.

**Content strands:** Number and Algebra (9 topics) · Geometry and Measurement
(7 topics) · Statistics and Probability (2 topics).

**Supplied at exam:** mathematical formulae (compound interest, mensuration,
trigonometry, statistics) and a full notation list — both printed in the syllabus
itself.

## SEAB-4049 — O-Level Additional Mathematics syllabus (examination 2026)

Source: https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/334/f4aaac1d-0d7f-492b-88d9-43e392418490/4049_y26_sy.pdf

**Assessment objectives:** AO1 standard techniques 35% · AO2 problems in context 50%
· AO3 reasoning/communication 15%.

**Scheme of assessment:** two papers, 2h15m each, 90 marks each (50/50), all
questions compulsory, calculators permitted both papers.
- Paper 1 — 12–14 questions, max 10 marks each.
- Paper 2 — 9–11 questions, max 12 marks each.

**Content strands:**
- Algebra (6): quadratic functions; equations and inequalities; surds; polynomials
  and partial fractions; binomial expansions; exponential and logarithmic functions.
- Geometry and Trigonometry (3): trig functions/identities/equations; coordinate
  geometry (2D); proofs in plane geometry.
- Calculus (1): differentiation and integration.

**Supplied at exam:** formulae list and notation reference covering algebra,
trigonometry, functions, exponential/log, circular functions, complex numbers,
matrices, vectors, probability/statistics.

## SEAB-8865 — H1 Mathematics syllabus (examination 2026)

Source: https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/334/ee60af06-7c13-484f-8f0c-1e37f3d2937c/8865_y26_sy.pdf

**Assessment objectives:** AO1 techniques/procedures 40% · AO2 formulate/solve
real-world problems 55% · AO3 reasoning/communication 5%.

**Scheme of assessment:** single paper, 3h, 100 marks.
- Section A — Pure Mathematics, ~40 marks, 5 questions.
- Section B — Probability & Statistics, 60 marks, 6–8 questions.
- One integrated application question, ≥12 marks, real-world business/social-science
  context spanning multiple topics.

**Calculator policy:** approved graphing calculator, no CAS; unsupported
calculator-only answers generally accepted unless a question says otherwise; working
must use mathematical notation, not calculator commands.

**Content:**
- Functions and graphs (exponential/log, graphing, equations/inequalities).
- Calculus (differentiation incl. chain rule and stationary points; integration incl.
  area between curves).
- Probability & Statistics (permutations/combinations/conditional probability;
  binomial distribution; normal distribution; sampling & CLT; hypothesis testing;
  correlation & linear regression).

**Formula list:** supplied at the exam; this syllabus text does not itself name MF27,
though MF27 (fetched separately, see below) covers H1/H2/H3/Further Maths from 2025.

## SEAB-9758 — H2 Mathematics syllabus (examination 2026)

Source: https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/334/f27e37f7-f0ec-4a35-b1e8-3a5e88ae2f81/9758_y26_sy.pdf

**Assessment objectives:** AO1 techniques/procedures 30% · AO2 formulate/solve
real-world problems 60% · AO3 reasoning/communication 10%.

**Scheme of assessment:** two papers, 3h each, 100 marks each (50/50), all questions
compulsory.
- Paper 1 — Pure Mathematics, 10–12 questions incl. one ≥12-mark application question.
- Paper 2 — Section A Pure Mathematics (40 marks, 4–5 questions) + Section B
  Probability & Statistics (60 marks, 6–8 questions incl. one ≥12-mark application
  question).

**Calculator policy:** same as H1 — approved graphing calculator, no CAS, working
must show mathematical notation not calculator syntax.

**Content (full list):**
- Functions & Graphs — domain/range/inverse/composite, graph features, standard
  curves, transformations, parametric equations.
- Sequences & Series — AP/GP (nth term, sums, convergence of infinite GP).
- Vectors — operations, position/displacement vectors, scalar & vector products, 3D
  lines/planes, distances and angles, parallel/intersecting/skew relationships.
- Complex Numbers — Cartesian form only (polar/exponential excluded at H2), modulus,
  argument, conjugate, Argand diagram, geometric effect of operations.
- Calculus — differentiation (incl. implicit/parametric, optimisation, related
  rates), Maclaurin series (standard expansions, small-angle approximations),
  integration techniques (substitution, by parts, standard forms), definite
  integrals (area, volume of revolution), separable differential equations.
- Probability & Statistics — counting/permutations/combinations, conditional
  probability, discrete random variables, binomial distribution, normal
  distribution, sampling & CLT (n≥30), hypothesis testing, correlation & linear
  regression (incl. linearising transformations).

**Formula/notation list:** syllabus explicitly states candidates are given a list of
formulae and results at the exam; a full notation section is printed in the syllabus
(pp.16–21 per the source). No explicit MF27 citation in this document's own text.

## SEAB-0008 — PSLE Mathematics (Standard) examination format

Source: https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/334/fe51f29b-04ae-4fcb-a907-8db04c028510/0008_y26_sy.pdf

**Assessment objectives:** AO1 recall facts/concepts/procedures · AO2 interpret and
apply in a variety of contexts · AO3 reason, analyse, select strategies.

**Scheme of assessment:** two papers, 100 marks total, 2h30m total (with a break).
- Paper 1 — 1h10m, no calculator. Booklet A is entirely MULTIPLE-CHOICE: 10 at 1 mark
  + 8 at 2 marks = 26 marks. Booklet B: 12 short-answer (2 marks) = 24 marks. Subtotal 50.
- **Corrected 12 Sep 2026.** The first extraction labelled Booklet A's eight 2-mark
  questions as short-answer. They are multiple-choice; MCQ carry both 1 and 2 marks.
- Paper 2 — 1h20m, calculator permitted. 5 short-answer (2 marks) + 10
  structured/long-answer (3–5 marks) = 50 marks.

**Content strands / notation:** not stated in this particular document — it's the
assessment-format paper, not the content syllabus; content strands for Primary are
covered separately in the MOE Primary curriculum document already in
`official-curriculum/moe-p.pdf`.

## SEAB-0038 — PSLE Mathematics (Foundation) examination format

Source: https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/334/5c51824e-16d2-47cf-bf64-ea5774beca04/0038_y26_sy.pdf

**Assessment objectives:** same three AOs as Standard, explicitly scaled down —
"simple contexts" / "simple situations" rather than "a variety of contexts."

**Scheme of assessment:** two papers, three booklets, 80 marks total, 1h45m total.
- Paper 1 — 1h, no calculator. **46 marks** (multiple-choice at 1 and 2 marks, plus
  short-answer at 2 marks).
- Paper 2 — 45min, calculator allowed. **34 marks**.
- **Corrected 12 Sep 2026.** The first extraction said 30 marks for Paper 1 and 50 for
  Paper 2. Both wrong; only 46/34 is consistent with the 80-mark total. The
  per-question-type counts remain unconfirmed against the printed page-2 table and must
  be checked by a person before use as evaluation truth.

**Versus Standard (0008):** fewer marks (80 vs 100), shorter overall time
(1h45m vs 2h30m), explicitly simplified context language in the AOs. The fetched
page didn't carry a side-by-side content comparison — if a full Foundation vs.
Standard topic diff is needed later, that's a follow-up fetch against the MOE
Primary curriculum doc rather than this format sheet.

## SEAB-MF27 — List of Formulae and Results (for use from 2025)

Source: https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/334/34bda122-a7f7-484f-9b46-9c4ad006fa21/SEAB_Mathematics_MF27__2025__.pdf

Published jointly by SEAB / Cambridge Assessment / MOE, for use in **all papers for
H1, H2, H3 Mathematics and H2 Further Mathematics** from 2025. This is the actual
sheet handed to JC students in the exam room — a Math agent should never "teach" a
formula from this list as something the student needs to memorise, and should say so
if asked.

**Contents:** algebraic series (binomial & Maclaurin expansions) · trigonometry
(angle formulae, inverse functions) · calculus (standard derivatives & integrals) ·
vectors (position vectors, vector products) · applications (arc length, surface area
of revolution) · numerical methods (trapezium rule, Newton–Raphson, Euler method) ·
statistics (discrete/continuous distributions, sampling, regression, Wilcoxon test)
· mathematical inequalities (AM–GM, Cauchy–Schwarz, triangle inequality,
inclusion–exclusion).
