# Mathematics syllabus map (Singapore)

Official MOE/SEAB **learning-outcome maps** for RAG. Not encyclopedic notes or worked solutions.

Sources: MOE Primary Mathematics Syllabus 2021 (P1 to P6, updated October 2025); MOE 2020 G1 Mathematics; MOE 2020 G2 and G3 Mathematics; MOE 2020 G2 and G3 Additional Mathematics; SEAB GCE O-Level 2026 Mathematics 4052 and Additional Mathematics 4049; SEAB GCE A-Level 2026 H1 Mathematics 8865 and H2 Mathematics 9758.

In 2025 the 2021 primary syllabus applies to P1–P5; P6 uses the 2013 syllabus until 2026. Foundation Mathematics is a reduced primary track (P5–P6). G1 is a reduced secondary core; G2/G3 feed O-Level 4052. Additional Mathematics feeds O-Level 4049.

## Primary Mathematics — Whole numbers (MOE Primary Mathematics Syllabus 2021, updated Oct 2025)

Strand: Number and Algebra. Learning outcomes by level:

- P1: numbers up to 100; counting, notation, place values (tens, ones); reading and writing numerals and words; comparing and ordering; patterns; ordinal numbers to tenth; addition and subtraction within 100 (including mental calculation within 20); concepts of multiplication and division; multiplying within 40; dividing within 20.
- P2: numbers up to 1000; odd and even numbers; addition and subtraction algorithms up to 3 digits; multiplication tables of 2, 3, 4, 5 and 10; relationship between multiplication and division.
- P3: numbers up to 10 000; multiplication tables of 6, 7, 8 and 9; division with remainder; multiplication and division algorithms (up to 3 digits by 1 digit).
- P4: numbers up to 100 000; rounding to the nearest 10, 100 or 1000; factors and multiples (including common factors and common multiples); multiplication up to 4 digits by 1 digit and up to 3 digits by 2 digits; division up to 4 digits by 1 digit.
- P5: numbers up to 10 million; multiplying and dividing by 10, 100, 1000 and their multiples; order of operations and brackets (without calculator).
- P5 Foundation: numbers up to 10 million with a reduced operations load.

Differentiation, algebra beyond P6 expressions, and Maclaurin series are not in the Primary syllabus.

## Primary Mathematics — Fractions (MOE Primary Mathematics Syllabus 2021, updated Oct 2025)

Strand: Number and Algebra, sub-strand Fractions.

- P2: fraction as part of a whole; notation; comparing and ordering unit and like fractions with denominators not exceeding 12; adding and subtracting like fractions within one whole.
- P3: equivalent fractions; simplest form; comparing unlike fractions with denominators not exceeding 12; adding and subtracting two related fractions within one whole.
- P4: mixed numbers and improper fractions; fraction as part of a set; adding and subtracting fractions with denominators not exceeding 12 and not more than two different denominators.
- P5: dividing a whole number by a whole number with quotient as a fraction; expressing fractions as decimals; adding and subtracting mixed numbers; multiplying proper/improper fractions and mixed numbers.
- P6: dividing a proper fraction by a whole number; dividing a whole number or proper fraction by a proper fraction.

## Primary Mathematics — Decimals, percentage, ratio, rate and money (MOE Primary Mathematics Syllabus 2021, updated Oct 2025)

- P1 money: counting cents up to $1 and dollars up to $100.
- P2 money: dollars and cents; decimal notation; comparing amounts; converting dollars and cents.
- P3 money: adding and subtracting money in decimal notation.
- P4 decimals: up to 3 decimal places; place values tenths, hundredths, thousandths; comparing; fractions to decimals (denominator a factor of 10 or 100); rounding to whole number, 1 d.p. or 2 d.p.; adding/subtracting up to 2 d.p.; multiplying/dividing decimals up to 2 d.p. by a 1-digit whole number.
- P5 decimals: multiplying/dividing up to 3 d.p. by 10, 100, 1000; converting compound metric units.
- P5 percentage: expressing a part as a percentage; finding a percentage of a whole; discount, GST and annual interest.
- P5 rate: amount of a quantity per unit of another quantity.
- P6 percentage: finding the whole given a part and the percentage; percentage increase/decrease.
- P6 ratio: a:b and a:b:c with whole numbers; equivalent ratios; dividing a quantity in a given ratio; simplest form; relationship between fraction and ratio.

## Primary Mathematics — Algebra (MOE Primary Mathematics Syllabus 2021, updated Oct 2025)

Algebra begins at P6 (Standard): using a letter to represent an unknown; simple algebraic expressions such as a ± 3, 3a, a ÷ 3; simplifying simple linear expressions excluding brackets; evaluating by substitution; simple linear equations with whole-number coefficients only.

This is not O-Level algebra (indices, simultaneous equations, quadratic factorisation) and not A-Level functions.

## Primary Mathematics — Measurement, area and volume (MOE Primary Mathematics Syllabus 2021, updated Oct 2025)

- P1 length in cm; time to 5 minutes; am/pm; duration of one hour/half hour.
- P2 length in m; mass in kg/g; volume of liquid in litres; time to the minute; converting hours and minutes.
- P3 length in km; volume in ml; compound units; 24-hour clock; starting/finishing time and duration; area and perimeter of rectilinear figures, rectangles and squares (cm² and m², no conversion between them).
- P4 area/perimeter of composite rectangles and squares; one missing dimension given area or perimeter.
- P5 area of triangle; composite figures of rectangles, squares and triangles; volume of cube/cuboid in cm³/m³; volume of liquid in a rectangular tank; litres and cm³.
- P6 area and circumference of a circle; semicircle and quarter circle; composite figures; missing cuboid dimensions given volume.

## Primary Mathematics — Geometry (MOE Primary Mathematics Syllabus 2021, updated Oct 2025)

- P1 2D shapes: rectangle, square, triangle, circle, half circle, quarter circle; forming figures; copying on grids.
- P2 2D patterns by size, shape, colour, orientation; 3D shapes: cube, cuboid, cone, cylinder, sphere.
- P3 angles (right angles, greater/smaller than a right angle); perpendicular and parallel lines.
- P4 measuring and drawing angles in degrees; properties of rectangle and square (excluding diagonals); line symmetry; nets of cube, cuboid, prism and pyramid.
- P5 angles on a straight line, at a point, vertically opposite; properties of isosceles, equilateral and right-angled triangles; angle sum of a triangle; parallelogram, rhombus and trapezium.
- P6 unknown angles in composite figures involving square, rectangle, triangle, parallelogram, rhombus and trapezium.

## Primary Mathematics — Statistics (MOE Primary Mathematics Syllabus 2021, updated Oct 2025)

- P1 picture graphs.
- P2 picture graphs with scales.
- P3 bar graphs, including different scales on an axis.
- P4 tables, line graphs and pie charts.
- P6 data analysis: average of a set of data.

Heuristics named in the primary curriculum include drawing a model, guess and check, work backwards, and make a supposition.

## Secondary G3 Mathematics — Number and algebra by level (MOE 2020 G2 and G3 Mathematics Syllabuses)

Teaching syllabus for Secondary One to Four (G3). Examined content is SEAB O-Level 4052.

Secondary One: primes and prime factorisation; HCF and LCM; squares, cubes, roots; integers, rational and real numbers; inequalities; approximation and estimation; ratio; percentage (including reverse percentages); rate and speed (including km/h to m/s); algebraic expressions and formulae; linear functions y = ax + b; linear equations in one variable.

Secondary Two: map scales; direct and inverse proportion; expansion of algebraic products; changing the subject of a formula; identities (a + b)², (a − b)², a² − b²; factorisation of quadratic expressions; quadratic functions and graphs; simultaneous linear equations; quadratic equations by factorisation.

Secondary Three/Four: standard form A × 10ⁿ; indices including fractional indices and laws of indices; algebraic fractions; functions and graphs of power and exponential functions; sketching quadratics; solving quadratics by formula and completing the square; linear inequalities; set language and Venn diagrams; matrices.

## Secondary G3 Mathematics — Geometry, mensuration, statistics (MOE 2020 G2 and G3 Mathematics Syllabuses)

Secondary One: angles, triangles and polygons; parallel lines and a transversal; construction; mensuration of parallelogram, trapezium, prism and cylinder.

Secondary Two: congruence and similarity; Pythagoras’ theorem; trigonometric ratios of acute angles; volume and surface area of pyramid, cone and sphere; probability of single events; mean, mode and median.

Secondary Three/Four: properties of circles (symmetry and angle properties); trigonometric ratios for obtuse angles; area of a triangle (½ ab sin C); sine rule and cosine rule; bearings; angles of elevation and depression; arc length, sector and segment; radian measure; coordinate geometry of the straight line; vectors in two dimensions; combined events and tree diagrams; standard deviation; cumulative frequency; box-and-whisker plots.

G2 Mathematics covers the same three strands at reduced depth. G3 content for Sec 5 students taking G2 Mathematics is listed separately in the MOE document. G1 Mathematics (MOE 2020 G1 Mathematics Syllabus) is a further-reduced core across Number and Algebra, Geometry and Measurement, and Statistics and Probability for Secondary One to Four.

## O-Level Mathematics 4052 — Number and algebra (SEAB 2026)

Subject content N1–N9. Candidates should know:

- N1 Numbers: primes and prime factorisation; HCF and LCM; squares, cubes and roots; negative numbers, integers, rational and real numbers; number line; inequalities; approximation and estimation; standard form A × 10ⁿ where 1 ≤ A < 10; positive, negative, zero and fractional indices; laws of indices.
- N2 Ratio and proportion: ratios of rational numbers; simplest form; map scales (distance and area); direct and inverse proportion.
- N3 Percentage: one quantity as a percentage of another; percentages greater than 100%; reverse percentages.
- N4 Rate and speed: average rate and average speed; conversion of units (e.g. km/h to m/s).
- N5 Algebraic expressions and formulae: letters for numbers; linear expressions; factorisation of ax + bx + kay + kby and of ax² + bx + c; expansion; changing the subject; algebraic fractions.
- N6 Functions and graphs: Cartesian coordinates; linear and quadratic functions; gradient; power functions y = axⁿ (n = −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, 3); exponential y = kaˣ; estimating gradient by a tangent.
- N7 Equations and inequalities: linear equations; simultaneous linear equations; quadratic equations by factorisation, formula, completing the square and graphically; linear inequalities on the number line.
- N8 Set language and Venn diagrams.
- N9 Matrices: display, scalar multiple, sum and product where appropriate.

Maclaurin series, complex numbers, and 3D vector products are not in 4052.

## O-Level Mathematics 4052 — Geometry, measurement, statistics and probability (SEAB 2026)

- G1 Angles, triangles and polygons: types of angles; parallel lines; properties of triangles, special quadrilaterals and regular polygons; constructions.
- G2 Congruence and similarity: similar triangles and polygons; scale drawings; perpendicular and angle bisectors; ratios of areas of similar figures and volumes of similar solids.
- G3 Properties of circles: equal chords; tangent from an external point; angle in a semicircle; angle in alternate segment; angle at the centre; opposite segments supplementary.
- G4 Pythagoras’ theorem and trigonometry: sine, cosine and tangent of acute angles; obtuse sine and cosine; ½ ab sin C; sine rule and cosine rule; 2D/3D problems including elevation, depression and bearings.
- G5 Mensuration: parallelogram and trapezium; composite plane figures; volume and surface area of cube, cuboid, prism, cylinder, pyramid, cone and sphere; arc length, sector and segment; radian measure.
- G6 Coordinate geometry: gradient and length of a line segment; y = mx + c.
- G7 Vectors in two dimensions: column vectors, position vectors, magnitude, sum, difference, scalar multiple.
- S1 Data handling: tables, bar graphs, pictograms, line graphs, pie charts, histograms, stem-and-leaf, cumulative frequency, box-and-whisker; mean, mode, median; quartiles; range, interquartile range and standard deviation.
- S2 Probability: single and simple combined events; possibility diagrams and tree diagrams; mutually exclusive and independent events.

Problems in real-world contexts include simple and compound interest, taxation, instalments, utilities and money exchange.

## O-Level Additional Mathematics 4049 — Algebra (SEAB 2026)

Assumes O-Level Mathematics 4052. Algebra strand:

- A1 Quadratic functions: completing the square for maximum/minimum; conditions for ax² + bx + c always positive or always negative; quadratics as models.
- A2 Equations and inequalities: discriminant (two real roots, equal roots, no real roots); line intersecting, tangent to, or missing a curve; simultaneous equations (one linear); quadratic inequalities on the number line.
- A3 Surds: four operations; rationalising the denominator; equations involving surds.
- A4 Polynomials and partial fractions: remainder and factor theorems; cubic equations; a³ ± b³ identities; partial fractions with denominators (ax + b)(cx + d), (ax + b)(cx + d)², (ax + b)(x² + c²).
- A5 Binomial expansions: Binomial Theorem for positive integer n; n! and ⁿCᵣ; general term (knowledge of the greatest term is not required).
- A6 Exponential and logarithmic functions: aˣ, eˣ, log_a x, ln x and their graphs; laws of logarithms; change of base; equations; functions as models.

## O-Level Additional Mathematics 4049 — Differentiation, integration, geometry and trigonometry (SEAB 2026)

- G1 Trigonometric functions, identities and equations: six trigonometric functions for any magnitude (degrees or radians); principal values of sin⁻¹ x, cos⁻¹ x, tan⁻¹ x; exact values for 30°, 45°, 60°; graphs of sine, cosine and tangent with amplitude and period; compound-angle and double-angle formulae; R sin(θ ± α) form; simple trigonometric equations in a given interval (excluding general solution); proofs of simple identities.
- G2 Coordinate geometry in two dimensions: parallel and perpendicular lines; midpoint; area of a rectilinear figure; circles (x − a)² + (y − b)² = r² and x² + y² + 2gx + 2fy + c = 0 (excluding two circles); transforming y = axⁿ and y = kbˣ to linear form.
- G3 Proofs in plane geometry: parallel lines, congruent and similar triangles, midpoint theorem, tangent-chord (alternate segment) theorem.
- C1 Differentiation and integration: derivative as gradient of the tangent and as rate of change; derivatives of xⁿ (rational n), sin x, cos x, tan x, eˣ, ln x; products, quotients and chain rule; stationary points; second derivative test; tangents, normals, connected rates, maxima and minima; integration as reverse of differentiation; definite integral as area under a curve (excluding area between two curves); kinematics of a particle in a straight line (displacement, velocity, acceleration).

Maclaurin series, differential equations, and 3D vectors are not in 4049.

## Secondary Additional Mathematics — Teaching syllabus (MOE 2020 G2 and G3 Additional Mathematics)

G3 Additional Mathematics (Secondary Three to Four) matches the 4049 strands Algebra, Geometry and Trigonometry, and Calculus, including C1 differentiation and integration. G2 Additional Mathematics is a reduced version of the same strands. A Secondary Five G2 top-up list is in the MOE document. Use this teaching map with SEAB 4049 for examined outcomes.

## A-Level H1 Mathematics 8865 — Functions, graphs and calculus (SEAB 2026)

H1 Mathematics supports business and social sciences. It is appropriate for students without O-Level Additional Mathematics. Graphing calculator expected. Pure Mathematics (Section A) includes:

- Functions and graphs: function as a unique-output rule; eˣ and ln x and their graphs; exponential growth and decay; logarithmic growth; laws of logarithms; graph characteristics (symmetry, intercepts, turning points, horizontal and vertical asymptotes). Excluded: finding domain and range; change of base.
- Equations and inequalities: discriminant of a quadratic; always positive/negative quadratics; simultaneous equations (one linear, one quadratic); quadratic inequalities analytically; inequalities graphically; systems of linear equations from a problem situation.
- Differentiation: derivative as gradient of the tangent; derivatives of xⁿ, eˣ, ln x with constant multiples, sums and differences; chain rule; first or second derivative test for local max/min and points of inflexion in simple cases; tangents; local maxima and minima. Excluded: first principles; products and quotients; implicit or parametric differentiation.
- Integration: reverse of differentiation; xⁿ and eˣ; (ax + b)ⁿ and e^(ax+b); definite integral as area under a curve; area between a curve and lines, or between two curves. Excluded: area below the x-axis; trapezium rule.

Maclaurin series is not in H1 8865.

## A-Level H1 Mathematics 8865 — Probability and statistics (SEAB 2026)

Section B (60 of 100 marks):

- Probability: addition and multiplication principles for counting; permutations ⁿPᵣ and combinations ⁿCᵣ; arrangements of distinct objects in a line (with restriction); mutually exclusive and independent events; Venn and tree diagrams; conditional probability in simple cases.
- Binomial distribution B(n, p) as a probability model; mean and variance (without proof).
- Normal distribution N(μ, σ²); standard normal; P(X < x₁); E(aX + b), Var(aX + b); independent X and Y. Exclude normal approximation to binomial.
- Sampling: population and simple random sample; sample mean with E(X̄) = μ and Var(X̄) = σ²/n; Central Limit Theorem for n ⩾ 30; unbiased estimates of mean and variance.
- Hypothesis testing: H₀ and H₁, test statistic, critical region, level of significance, p-value; 1-tail and 2-tail tests for a population mean. Exclude Type I/II errors and two-mean tests.
- Correlation and linear regression: scatter diagram; product moment correlation coefficient; least squares; interpolation and extrapolation. Exclude hypothesis tests on correlation and transformation for linearity.

## A-Level H2 Mathematics 9758 — Functions, sequences, vectors and complex numbers (SEAB 2026)

Assumes O-Level Mathematics. Assumed Additional Mathematics knowledge is listed in the 9758 appendix (quadratics, surds, polynomials, logs, circles, trigonometric identities, differentiation and integration).

- Functions: domain and range; inverse and composite functions; conditions for existence; domain restriction to obtain an inverse; graphs of a one-to-one function and its inverse.
- Graphs and transformations: y = ax², x = by², ellipses and hyperbolas, y = (ax + b)/(cx + d); asymptotes and axes of symmetry; transformations y = af(x), y = f(x) + a, y = f(x + a), y = f(ax); graphs of |f(x)| and 1/f(x); simple parametric equations.
- Equations and inequalities: systems of linear equations; inequalities of the form f(x)/g(x) > 0; modulus inequalities |x − a| < b and |x − a| > b.
- Sequences and series: nth term and Sₙ; arithmetic series; finite geometric series; condition for convergence of an infinite geometric series and sum to infinity.
- Vectors in two and three dimensions: addition, scalar multiple, position vectors, magnitude, unit vectors, collinearity, ratio theorem; scalar product and vector product; vector and Cartesian equations of lines and planes; foot of the perpendicular; angles between lines and planes. Exclude shortest distance between skew lines.
- Complex numbers in Cartesian form: Argand diagrams; modulus, argument and conjugate; four operations; conjugate roots of real polynomials. Polar and exponential form are excluded.

## A-Level H2 Mathematics 9758 — Calculus including Maclaurin series (SEAB 2026)

- Differentiation: graphical interpretation of f' and f''; relating y = f'(x) to y = f(x); implicit and parametric differentiation; stationary points; tangents and normals (including implicit/parametric); connected rates of change. Exclude non-stationary points of inflexion and second derivative of parametric functions.
- Maclaurin series: standard expansions of (1 + x)ⁿ, eˣ, sin x, cos x and ln(1 + x); first few terms by repeated differentiation, repeated implicit differentiation, or standard series; range of convergence; Maclaurin’s series as an approximation of a function; small angle approximations sin x ≈ x, cos x ≈ 1 − ½x², tan x ≈ x. Derivation of the general term is excluded. Maclaurin series is H2 A-Level, not O-Level and not H1.
- Integration techniques: f'(x)[f(x)]ⁿ, f'(x)e^{f(x)}, sin² x, cos² x, tan² x; 1/(a² + x²) and 1/(a² − x²) forms; substitution; integration by parts. Exclude reduction formulae.
- Definite integrals: limit of sum; area under a curve including area below the x-axis; volume of revolution about the x- or y-axis.
- Differential equations of the form dy/dx = f(x)g(y), including a given substitution; formulating and interpreting a differential equation from a problem situation.

## A-Level H2 Mathematics 9758 — Probability and statistics (SEAB 2026)

- Probability: permutations and combinations; arrangements in a line or a circle (repetition and restriction); mutually exclusive and independent events; conditional probability.
- Discrete random variables: probability distributions, expectation and variance; binomial B(n, p). Exclude finding the c.d.f. of a discrete random variable.
- Normal distribution as for H1, still excluding normal approximation to binomial.
- Sampling, Central Limit Theorem (n ⩾ 30), unbiased estimates.
- Hypothesis testing for a population mean (1-tail and 2-tail). Exclude Type I/II errors and two-mean tests.
- Correlation and linear regression, including square, reciprocal or logarithmic transformation to achieve linearity.
