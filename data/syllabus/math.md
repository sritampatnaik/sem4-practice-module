# Mathematics syllabus map (Singapore)

This file stores **curriculum coverage**, not worked solutions. METS RAG should retrieve these headings when deciding what a student is expected to know.

## Primary

- Whole numbers, fractions, decimals, percentages, ratio, and rate.
- Measurement: length, mass, volume, time, money, area, and perimeter.
- Geometry: angles, triangles, quadrilaterals, circles, nets, and symmetry.
- Statistics: picture graphs, bar graphs, line graphs, and average.
- Algebra at P6: simple algebraic expressions in one variable.
- Heuristics: draw a model, guess and check, work backwards, make a supposition.

## Secondary (O-Level Mathematics / Additional Mathematics)

- Number and algebra: indices, standard form, equations, inequalities, simultaneous equations, functions, graphs.
- Geometry and measurement: congruence, similarity, Pythagoras, trigonometry in right-angled and non-right-angled triangles, bearings, mensuration of prisms, cylinders, pyramids, cones, spheres.
- Statistics and probability: data representation, mean/median/mode, probability of simple and combined events.
- Additional Mathematics: quadratic functions, surds, polynomials, binomial theorem, exponential and logarithmic functions, trigonometric identities, differentiation, integration, kinematics.

## JC (A-Level H1/H2 Mathematics)

- Functions, graphs, transformations, and inverse functions.
- Sequences and series, including AP/GP and binomial expansion.
- Vectors in two and three dimensions, scalar and vector products.
- Complex numbers in Cartesian and polar form.
- Calculus: differentiation, integration, Maclaurin series, differential equations (H2).
- Probability and statistics: permutations and combinations, discrete and continuous distributions, sampling, hypothesis testing, correlation and regression.
