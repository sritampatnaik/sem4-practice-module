# Physics syllabus map (Singapore)

This file stores **curriculum coverage**, not full derivations. Use it to keep explanations inside the student's band.

## Primary

- Diversity of materials and their properties.
- Cycles: life cycles, water cycle, and matter as solid, liquid, gas.
- Interactions: forces (pushes and pulls), magnets, energy in food chains (light science overlap).
- Energy: light, heat, and electrical systems at an introductory level.
- Measurement with standard units; fair tests and simple investigations.

## Secondary (O-Level Physics)

- Physical quantities, SI units, scalars and vectors.
- Kinematics, dynamics, turning effects, pressure, energy, work, and power.
- Thermal physics: kinetic model, transfer of thermal energy, temperature.
- Waves: general wave properties, light (reflection, refraction, lenses), electromagnetic spectrum, sound.
- Electricity and magnetism: current, voltage, resistance, D.C. circuits, practical electricity, magnetism, electromagnetism, electromagnetic induction.
- Radioactivity and the nuclear atom at O-Level depth.

## JC (A-Level H1/H2 Physics)

- Measurement, uncertainties, and graphical analysis.
- Newtonian mechanics, circular motion, gravitation, oscillations.
- Thermal physics and the first law; ideal gases.
- Superposition, interference, diffraction, and stationary waves.
- Electric fields, capacitance, current of electricity, electromagnetism, electromagnetic induction, alternating current.
- Quantum physics, wave-particle duality, nuclear physics.
- H2 practical skills: planning, analysis, and evaluation.
