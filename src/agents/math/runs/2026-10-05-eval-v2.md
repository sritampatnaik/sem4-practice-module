# Math eval v2: generated cases, 5 October 2026

Two runs of the Math suite on gpt-4o from the scores page, on `haixiang` at `3b8afb6` plus the changes below. A first attempt the same day failed on 64 of 67 cases because the OpenAI account had no credits; it is in the history log and is not counted.

## What was added

- `src/evals/catalog/math-generated.ts`: templates and a seeded generator (mulberry32). A spec → builder (`makeGeneratedItem`) turns each draw into an eval item, like `makeRoutingItem` in `routing.ts`. Every answer is computed by mathjs in exact Fraction mode, or follows from how the case was built. None comes from an LLM.
- 35 new cases, so the suite has 67:

| Layer | Cases | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Computed (`math-gen-computed-`) | 10 | `mustInclude: [answer]` plus the solver |
| Held-out (`math-holdout-`) | 10 | the same, drawn from a separate seed |
| Exact form | 4 | judge only; fraction or surd answers, titles say "(judge-scored)" |
| Hint only | 4 | `mustNotInclude: [answer]` |
| Planted error | 4 | code inserts one wrong step; `mustInclude: [correct answer]`, the judge checks the named step |
| Consistency | 3 | one arithmetic series asked as an AP, in sigma notation and as a word problem; one answer |

- Topics: trigonometric ratios, logarithm and exponential equations, linear inequalities, gradient and distance, mean and median, nCr and nPr, Primary percentage and ratio, AP and GP sums, definite integrals, and binomial probability. Each topic has one computed and one held-out case, in its syllabus band: 3 Primary, 9 Secondary and 8 JC.
- Answers are integers or short decimals with at least two digits, below 1000, and never appear in the prompt.
- Held-out cases use seed 77031, or `MATH_HOLDOUT_SEED` if it is set on the server, so they can be redrawn at report time. They were not used to change the prompt or the tools.
- `generated-evals.test.ts` (7 tests):
  - recomputes every computed and held-out answer from its prompt with plain arithmetic, over five seeds;
  - checks that no answer appears in its prompt;
  - checks that ids are fixed for a given seed;
  - checks the layer counts and the planted-error and consistency answers.

  All 41 Math tests pass.

## Limits

- The templates are cleaner than real student wording.
- Most generated cases exercise the solver. The first-step rule calls it for almost every prompt here, so these cases test arithmetic more than explanation.
- The judge still grades explanations. It is the same model family as the agent, and hint quality and planted-step naming rest on it alone.
- A literal check can pass on an incidental match, for example "10" inside another number.
- Two runs per layer are small samples, and four cases per layer cannot separate a rate of 75% from 100%.
