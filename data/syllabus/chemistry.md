# Chemistry syllabus map (Singapore)

This file stores **curriculum coverage** and assessment emphasis, not encyclopedic chemistry.

## Primary (PSLE Science - Chemistry-related topics)
*Note: Chemistry is not a standalone subject at the primary level; these fall under Primary Science themes like Diversity and Cycles.*

Reference: https://www.moe.gov.sg/api/media/ba3562d3-5b31-4459-8693-45cde7b97273/Primary-Science-Syllabus-2023.pdf

- **Diversity of Materials:** Classifying materials based on physical properties (hardness, strength, flexibility, transparency, heat/electrical conductivity). 
- **Cycles in Matter:** The three states of matter (solids, liquids, gases); mass and volume.
- **Water Cycle:** Melting, boiling, evaporation, freezing, and condensation.
- **Safety and Environment:** Conserving water, responsible use of materials.

## Lower Secondary (Secondary 1 & 2 Science - Chemistry Modules)
- **Exploring Matter:** Physical properties of matter, measuring density.
- **Chemical Changes:** Physical vs. chemical changes; observable signs of chemical reactions.
- **Separation Techniques:** Mixtures vs. compounds/elements, dissolving, solutions, solubility, and simple separation methods (filtration, distillation, paper chromatography).
- **Atoms and Molecules:** Basic particulate nature of matter, chemical models.
- **Acids and Alkalis:** Indicators, properties of acidic and alkaline solutions.

## Secondary (O-Level Chemistry)
Reference: https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/334/19dfb9f9-b3ce-4609-a3cf-5d30052317f5/6092_y26_sy.pdf

- **Experimental Chemistry:** Experimental design, apparatus, separation/purification techniques, qualitative analysis (identification of ions and gases).
- **Particulate Nature of Matter:** Kinetic particle theory, atomic structure, ionic, covalent, and metallic bonding, structure and properties of materials.
- **Formulae and Stoichiometry:** The mole concept, empirical/molecular formulae, reacting masses, volumetric analysis.
- **Chemistry of Reactions:** Acids, bases and salts, redox reactions, electrochemistry, chemical energetics, speed of reaction (kinetics).
- **Periodicity:** The Periodic Table, Group 1, Group 17, noble gases, transition elements, properties of metals and the reactivity series.
- **Atmosphere:** Air composition, environmental chemistry.
- **Organic Chemistry:** Fuels and crude oil, alkanes, alkenes, alcohols, carboxylic acids, and polymers (macromolecules).

## JC (A-Level H1 Chemistry)
Reference: https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/334/00e57932-74b9-4a60-b0c8-4a8e183e440c/8873_y26_sy.pdf

- **Core Ideas:** Matter, Structure and Properties, Transformation.
- **Atomic Structure & Bonding:** Atomic structure, chemical bonding (ionic, covalent, metallic), intermolecular forces.
- **Chemical Energetics:** Enthalpy changes.
- **Reaction Kinetics:** Rate of reaction, catalysts.
- **Chemical Equilibria:** Reversible reactions, Le Chatelier's principle, acid-base equilibria (pH, buffers).
- **Periodicity:** Period 3 elements.
- **Organic Chemistry:** Reduced scope compared to H2. Covers hydrocarbons (alkanes, alkenes), halogen derivatives, hydroxy compounds, carbonyl compounds, and carboxylic acids.
- *Note: H1 excludes Electrochemistry, Transition Elements, Nitrogen compounds, and Practical Assessment.*

## JC (A-Level H2 Chemistry)
Reference: https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/334/4dd1835f-d26b-4967-a4c5-657c92a60767/9476_y26_sy.pdf

- **Includes all H1 concepts at greater depth, plus:**
- **Advanced Physical Chemistry:** Electrochemistry, solubility product equilibria.
- **Advanced Inorganic Chemistry:** Group 2, Group 17, Chemistry of the Transition Elements.
- **Advanced Organic Chemistry:** Arenes (electrophilic substitution), nitrogen compounds (amines, amides, amino acids, proteins), electrophilic and nucleophilic mechanisms.
- **Practical Assessment:** Planning, manipulation, measurement, and data-based practical assessments.
