The worst of three runs is 24/26 (92%), so the target is met.

## What changed since run 4

- **`examFacts` tool:** SEAB exam-format facts for PSLE Standard and Foundation, 4052, 4049, 8865, 9758 and MF27. Each fact has its source and check date, and anything unconfirmed is listed as such. Questions about how an exam works now start from it. All five exam-fact cases pass on all three runs, and the A-Math calculator answer is now correct ("both Paper 1 and Paper 2").
- **Integral check:** the prompt says to check an integral by differentiating the answer, not the integrand. The false "tool verified" claim from run 4 has not recurred.
- **MF27 cross-reference:** two runs before the final three, the formula-sheet case hedged that MF27 "may include" the product rule, because the H2 entry didn't say what MF27 leaves out. The H1 and H2 entries now state it, and the case passed all three final runs.
- **No behaviour change intended:**
  - first-step rules moved into a table in `first-step.ts`, with their own tests;
  - overlapping prompt rules merged;
  - image-stripping patterns reduced from ten to six;
  - a shared safe-evaluate helper in `tools.ts`;
  - a test that the Langflow copy is on the same prompt version.

## Still failing

- **`math-topology-beyond-syllabus` (3 of 3 runs):** the model labels the topic correctly, then gives a university-level overview (simplicial complexes, Betti numbers) instead of the short orientation the prompt asks for. This is a real instruction-following gap, not a scoring fault. It's left as a known limitation rather than tuned to this case.
- **`math-calculus-primary` (1 of 3):** the model sometimes still offers "rates of change" to a Primary student.

## Limits

- The 26 cases are now a regression set; further gains on them would be overfitting.
- Generalisation needs a held-out set, and tutoring quality (misconceptions, follow-ups, hints) needs its own cases.
- The judge has not been calibrated against human marking.
