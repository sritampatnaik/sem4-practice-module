# Requirements of Graduate Certificate - 1

## Objective

In this graduate certificate, participants will learn to apply essential practices in Explainable and Responsible AI, AI-specific cybersecurity, Agentic AI design patterns, advanced software architecture skills, and MLSecOps/LLMSecOps to design, architect, implement and deploy AI systems that are responsible, ethical, explainable, robust, highly available, reusable, maintainable, and extensible, along with the development of relevant project artifacts.

## Outcomes

Demonstrate **competency** in all **four** course modules.

### Module 1: Explainable and Responsible Artificial Intelligence (3 Days)

The main objective of this course is to equip participants with practical skills to design and implement AI systems that are accountable, transparent, fair, and ethically responsible. It also aims to develop their ability to assess model fairness, apply bias reduction strategies, use Explainable AI tools, and apply responsible AI frameworks across real-world scenarios.

### Module 2: AI and Cybersecurity (3 days)

This course provides cybersecurity and digital professionals with the knowledge and skills to identify vulnerabilities in AI systems, evaluate AI's role in cybersecurity defense, and understand AI-powered attack techniques. Participants will also learn to formulate security strategies that address the risks in AI-based systems.

### Module 3: Architecting Agentic AI Solutions (4 days)

By combining hands-on experience with system-level thinking, this course prepares professionals to lead the architecture, design and implementation of next-generation AI solutions driven by autonomous, multi-agent systems.

### Module 4: Integrating and Deploying AI Solutions (3 days)

This course provides comprehensive coverage on the principles, methods, and tools for integrating and deploying real-world systems that incorporate intelligent components. Participants will learn MLSecOps and LLMSecOps to streamline the entire ML/LLM lifecycle.
