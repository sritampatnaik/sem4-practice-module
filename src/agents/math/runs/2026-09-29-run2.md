## New defect

`math-graph-reciprocal` drew the graph with the tool correctly, then copied the tool's 241 plotted points into its reply as a fake `<jsxgraph ...>` tag. The cap stopped it at 2,000 tokens and the cut-short note appeared, but the student still sees a wall of numbers, and the eval still scored it 0.95.

The cause is that the model receives the full plotting data from the tool. The general fix:

1. Send the model a short summary (roots, turning points, asymptotes, domain), while the widget still gets the full data.
2. Strip tool-like markup in the guardrail.
3. Add an eval check that fails a reply containing raw graph data.

The cut-short note also needs a paragraph break before it.
