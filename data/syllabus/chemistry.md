# Chemistry syllabus map (Singapore)

This file stores **curriculum coverage** and assessment emphasis, not encyclopedic chemistry.

## Primary

- Characteristics of materials; classifying things as living or non-living is science, not chemistry-specific.
- Matter: solids, liquids, gases; melting, boiling, evaporation, condensation.
- Mixtures and solutions; dissolving; reversible vs irreversible changes at a basic level.
- Safety: never taste chemicals; report spills; follow teacher instructions.

## Secondary (O-Level Chemistry)

- Experimental chemistry: apparatus, purity, separation techniques, identification of ions and gases.
- Particulate nature of matter, atomic structure, ionic and covalent bonding, metallic bonding.
- Stoichiometry, the mole, empirical and molecular formulae, reacting masses, volumetric analysis.
- Acids, bases, salts; qualitative analysis; the periodic table; Group I, VII, noble gases, transition metals at O-Level depth.
- Energy from chemicals; redox; metals and reactivity series; electrochemistry (electrolysis).
- Speed of reaction; ammonia; atmosphere and environment.
- Organic chemistry: fuels, alkanes, alkenes, alcohols, carboxylic acids, polymers, macromolecules.

## JC (A-Level H1/H2 Chemistry)

- Atomic structure, chemical bonding (including hybridisation and VSEPR at H2), chemical energetics.
- Equilibria: chemical, ionic, solubility product; acid-base equilibria.
- Reaction kinetics and electrochemistry at A-Level depth.
- Periodicity, Group 2, Group 17, transition elements (H2).
- Organic chemistry: hydrocarbons, halogen derivatives, hydroxy compounds, carbonyl compounds, carboxylic acids and derivatives, nitrogen compounds, electrophilic and nucleophilic mechanisms.
- Planning and data-based practical assessment.
