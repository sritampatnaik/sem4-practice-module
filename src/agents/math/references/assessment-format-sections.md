## Primary assessment format (PSLE Standard Mathematics 0008)

Source: SEAB 0008, examined from 2026. Assessment format only; topic coverage is in the Primary section above.

- Assessment objectives: AO1 recall facts, concepts, rules and formulae and perform straightforward computation; AO2 interpret information and apply concepts and skills in a variety of contexts; AO3 reason mathematically, analyse information and select strategies.
- Two papers, 100 marks, 2 h 30 min total, taken the same day with a break.
- Paper 1 (1 h 10 min, **no calculator**): Booklet A is entirely multiple choice - 10 at 1 mark and 8 at 2 marks (26 marks); Booklet B 12 short answer at 2 marks (24 marks). Multiple-choice questions carry both 1 and 2 marks.
- Paper 2 (1 h 20 min, **calculator allowed**): 5 short answer at 2 marks and 10 structured or long answer at 3-5 marks (50 marks).
- Short and structured answers credit correct method.

## Primary assessment format (PSLE Foundation Mathematics 0038)

Source: SEAB 0038, examined from 2026. Foundation is a separate assessment, not a harder or easier version of Standard; its assessment objectives scope application and reasoning to simple contexts and simple situations.

- Two papers across three booklets, 80 marks, 1 h 45 min total.
- Paper 1 (1 h, **no calculator**): **46 marks**, comprising multiple-choice questions at 1 and 2 marks and short-answer questions at 2 marks.
- Paper 2 (45 min, **calculator allowed**): **34 marks**, short-answer and structured questions.
- As in the Standard paper, multiple-choice questions carry both 1 and 2 marks.
- The per-question-type counts are not yet confirmed against SEAB 0038 page 2; the mark totals above are. Corrected 12 Sep 2026 (was wrongly 30/50).

## Secondary assessment format (O-Level Mathematics 4052)

Source: SEAB 4052, examination 2026. Assessment format only; topic coverage is in the Secondary section above.

- Assessment objectives and weightings: AO1 use and apply standard techniques **45%**; AO2 solve problems in a variety of contexts **40%**; AO3 reason and communicate mathematically **15%**.
- Two papers, 90 marks each, 50% each.
- Paper 1: 2 h 15 min, about 26 shorter-answer questions.
- Paper 2: 2 h 15 min, 9-10 questions of varying length, with emphasis on real-world contexts.
- The syllabus prints a list of formulae (compound interest, mensuration, trigonometry, statistics) and a notation list. Formulae on that list are provided in the examination and need not be memorised.

## Secondary assessment format (O-Level Additional Mathematics 4049)

Source: SEAB 4049, examination 2026.

- Assessment objectives and weightings: AO1 use and apply standard techniques **35%**; AO2 solve problems in a variety of contexts **50%**; AO3 reason and communicate mathematically **15%**.
- Two papers, 2 h 15 min each, 90 marks each, 50% each. All questions compulsory. Calculator permitted in **both** papers.
- Paper 1: 12-14 questions of varying length, maximum 10 marks each.
- Paper 2: 9-11 questions of varying length, maximum 12 marks each.
- Content strands: Algebra (quadratic functions; equations and inequalities; surds; polynomials and partial fractions; binomial expansions; exponential and logarithmic functions); Geometry and Trigonometry (trigonometric functions, identities and equations; coordinate geometry in two dimensions; proofs in plane geometry); Calculus (differentiation and integration).

## JC assessment format (H1 Mathematics 8865)

Source: SEAB 8865, examination 2026.

- Assessment objectives and weightings: AO1 use mathematical techniques and procedures **40%**; AO2 formulate and solve problems including real-world contexts **55%**; AO3 reason and communicate mathematically **5%**.
- One paper, 3 hours, 100 marks. Section A Pure Mathematics about 40 marks across 5 questions; Section B Probability and Statistics 60 marks across 6-8 questions. At least one application question of 12 marks or more, set in a real-world business or social-science context.
- Approved graphing calculator expected, no computer algebra system. Unsupported answers from the calculator are generally allowed unless a question states otherwise, and working must be shown in mathematical notation rather than calculator commands.

## JC assessment format (H2 Mathematics 9758)

Source: SEAB 9758, examination 2026.

- Assessment objectives and weightings: AO1 use mathematical techniques and procedures **30%**; AO2 formulate and solve problems including real-world contexts **60%**; AO3 reason and communicate mathematically **10%**.
- Two papers, 3 hours and 100 marks each, 50% each, all questions compulsory.
- Paper 1: Pure Mathematics, 10-12 questions of varying length, including one application question of at least 12 marks.
- Paper 2: Section A Pure Mathematics 40 marks (4-5 questions); Section B Probability and Statistics 60 marks (6-8 questions), including one application question of at least 12 marks.
- Approved graphing calculator required, no computer algebra system. Working must use mathematical notation, not calculator commands.
- Complex numbers are examined in Cartesian form; polar and exponential forms are excluded at H2.

## JC formula sheet supplied in the examination (MF27)

Source: SEAB List of Formulae and Results MF27, for use from 2025 in all papers for H1, H2 and H3 Mathematics and H2 Further Mathematics.

MF27 is given to candidates in the examination room, so formulae on it need not be memorised. It covers algebraic series (binomial, Maclaurin), trigonometry, standard derivatives and integrals, vectors, arc length and surface area of revolution, numerical methods (trapezium, Newton-Raphson, Euler), statistics (distributions, sampling, regression, Wilcoxon) and inequalities (AM-GM, Cauchy-Schwarz, triangle, inclusion-exclusion).

Results not on MF27 still need to be known, including the product, quotient and chain rules for differentiation.

MF27 serves H1, H2, H3 and Further Mathematics together, so a formula on it does **not** establish the topic is in a given course. Check that course's own section.
